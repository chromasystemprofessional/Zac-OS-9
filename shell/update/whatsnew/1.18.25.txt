ZacOS 9 1.18.25 makes Front Desk Setup a control panel, so an administrator
can open it from Control Panels or the Zac menu before any account runs
Front Desk.

The Users panel's Use only chosen programs now works for every account,
including administrators and your own. Programs… lists ZacOS's user
programs and leaves out control panels, installers and system programs.
Update Front Desk in Software > Extras (0.1.2 or later) for the control
panel and the corrected program list.
