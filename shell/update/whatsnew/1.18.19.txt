ZacOS 9 1.18.19 brings Front Desk, a shared-computer session shell for
chosen accounts.

Front Desk uses the real ZacOS accounts and the existing Use only chosen
programs list. Front Desk Setup chooses the programs, menus, save locations
and Finder access, and the login window wears the Front Desk welcome. Each
person's folder pages start at Home and are their own list.

Install Front Desk from Software > Extras after updating.
