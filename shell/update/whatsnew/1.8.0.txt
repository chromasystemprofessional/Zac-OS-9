ZacOS 9 now speaks Italian, Japanese and Russian.

Italian is the first full catalog: control panels, menus and Hint Bubbles,
Finder dialogs, the installer and Setup Assistant, the everyday
applications, and the whole ZacOS Help book and tutorial. Japanese and
Russian are previews: Russian covers the control panels, menu bar,
applications, installer and Setup Assistant, with its help book; Japanese
covers the control panels and displays Japanese correctly, including line
breaking. Japanese input is not included yet.

Each screen can now be rotated by 90, 180 or 270 degrees in the Monitors
panel, for a tablet or a monitor used on its side. The layout follows the
rotated shape, and each screen remembers its own rotation.

Help pages fall back to English page by page until a page is translated.
A native speaker's review of the catalogs is still to come.
