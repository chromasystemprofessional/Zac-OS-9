ZacOS 9 1.18.29 lets a Front Desk account choose Show all Zac menu items in
Front Desk Setup (Front Desk 0.1.5 or later in Software > Extras). The menu
bar's Zac menu then keeps its usual items—Control Panels, Software, Old Dog
and the rest—instead of only About This Computer. It is off by default
because the full menu is a way out of the panel.
