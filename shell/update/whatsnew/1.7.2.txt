Printing now tells you what went wrong. A failed document is named
in the message, and the rest of the batch still prints. A queue says
why a document is waiting or stopped: out of paper, held for an
administrator, or the printer stopped. If the printing service (CUPS)
isn't running, ZacOS tells you, instead of claiming no printer is
chosen.

Printer Picker now lists printers found on your network even before
you first print to them, including a Wi-Fi printer that may already
be your default. A printer that answers over both USB and Wi-Fi is
one row: the connection you chose is kept, and its other connections
are named in the details. The Collar's Printer menu still lists every
connection if you want a different one.

Themes now load completely. The Apple menu icon, disk icons, desktop
picture, alert sounds and fonts all come from the theme, and a missing
piece no longer stops the rest. Themes made for older versions of
ZacOS are recognised again. Theme files and the folder they are filed
into wear the theme's own icon; archives such as zip, 7z and StuffIt
files have a zipped-page icon.

Title-bar art now scales with the theme, and imported sound sets are
padded so they no longer clip. In the Sniffer, clicking one of several
selected icons selects only that icon.

Night Mode has been removed from Extras: it never worked properly.
Fetch Updates now checks for new versions of the ZacOS Extras you have
installed, as well as ZacOS 9 and Debian, and installs them with the
rest. The Steam extra now finds Steam installed from Flathub, lists
its games and starts them.
