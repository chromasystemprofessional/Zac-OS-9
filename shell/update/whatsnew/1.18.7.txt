ZacOS 9 1.18.7 fixes dragging a desktop icon after selecting another.

When one desktop icon was selected and you pressed and dragged a different
one, the earlier selection stayed behind and its icon came along for the
drag. Pressing the new icon now releases the old selection at once, so only
the icon under the pointer is dragged. Dragging a group that is already
selected still moves the whole group, as before.
