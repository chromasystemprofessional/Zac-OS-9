ZacOS 9 1.18.16 restores the bottom frame of imported Appearance themes.

iBook and Drawing Board draw their bottom border as a picture. It is
now extracted and tiled along the window bottom instead of leaving a
transparent gap. The same applies to any theme whose frame rail is a
picture rather than a colour strip, including its left and right rails.

Re-import the affected theme after updating so its bundle is rebuilt.
