ZacOS 9 1.18.28 splits the package for other distributions. zacos9-session
is the desktop session, zacos9-greeter the optional greetd login window, and
zacos9 the full system integration (boot, installer, Setup Assistant,
Software and updates). A distribution can install the session alone; the
full package depends on both pieces.

Front Desk sessions keep The Collar, and its hot key works there too. Update
Front Desk in Software > Extras (0.1.4 or later) for the panel's original
click sounds.
