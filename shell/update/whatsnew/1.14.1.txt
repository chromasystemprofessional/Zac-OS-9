Disks mounted from /etc/fstab now appear on the desktop.

An internal data disk added after installation is mounted by the system,
but GIO never reports it as a volume, so the Finder had no icon for it.
The Finder now finds those mounted block devices itself and shows each
one under its volume label — for example, ZacData for /mnt/data.

The disk is not ejectable and opens like any other disk icon.
