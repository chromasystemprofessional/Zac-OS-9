ZacOS 9 1.18.17 fixes Aqua's title bar and scrollbars.

Imported title bars now choose their pieces by name, not by record
order: a named left corner is the left cap, a named right tab is the
right cap, and the widest full-height record fills the bar. Aqua's
repeating endcap is gone.

Scrollbars use the theme's own track recipe and named thumb caps and
stretch. Aqua's 14 px thumb art is centred in the 16 px bar instead of
being stretched, and its top and bottom caps no longer collapse into a
blob.

Re-import the affected theme after updating so its bundle is rebuilt.
