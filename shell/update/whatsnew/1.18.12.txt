ZacOS 9 1.18.12 completes the Sniffer's Edit menu.

Cut (⌘X) now marks the selected files and folders to move, and Paste
(⌘V) moves them into the front window or Desktop. Copy (⌘C) puts them
on the Clipboard to duplicate instead; the copies are named "copy",
"copy 2" and so on.

Clear moves the selection to the Trash without putting it on the
Clipboard. The menu items are enabled only when there is something to
cut, copy, paste or clear, and files copied in another file manager can
be pasted in the Sniffer too.

Undo still belongs to the program in front; the Sniffer has none.
