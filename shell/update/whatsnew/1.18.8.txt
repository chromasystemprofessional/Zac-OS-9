ZacOS 9 1.18.8 brings Mac OS 9 theme sounds and controls together.

Appearance themes' Sound Sets now import with their resource forks, so
they appear in Sound Sets, and Kaleidoscope WindowShade sounds become a
selectable set too.

Kaleidoscope schemes with wnd# window recipes now draw their title bars
and widgets. Drawing Board's buttons, checkboxes, radios, progress bars
and scrollbars use the theme's faces, keeping ZacOS's geometry.

Software Update's What's New window now fits its notes to the panel.

Re-import your themes once, or open Appearance, to rebuild the bundles.
