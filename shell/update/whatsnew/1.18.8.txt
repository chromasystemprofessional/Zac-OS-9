ZacOS 9 1.18.8 brings Mac OS 9 theme sounds and controls together.

Appearance themes' Sound Sets now import with their resource forks, so the
sets appear in Sound Sets instead of only under Alert Sound. Kaleidoscope
schemes' WindowShade sounds become a selectable set too.

Kaleidoscope schemes that use wnd# window recipes now draw their title bars
and window widgets. Drawing Board's title art lines up, and its push
buttons, checkboxes, radio buttons, progress bars and scrollbars use the
theme's own faces while keeping ZacOS's geometry and labels.

Re-import your themes once, or open Appearance, so the updated extractor
rebuilds their bundles.
