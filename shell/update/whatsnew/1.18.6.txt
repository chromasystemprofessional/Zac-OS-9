ZacOS 9 1.18.6 lets programs installed from Flathub print.

Flatpak keeps the printing service out of a program's sandbox unless the
program asks for it. Adobe Reader, installed from Flathub, asked only for a
window, so its Print dialog had no printers in its Name list. At login ZacOS
now gives Flatpak programs the same printing service the rest of the desktop
uses. Restart a Flatpak program that was already open, and its Print dialog
lists the printers this computer knows.
