ZacOS 9 1.18.34 lets Front Desk give each screen its own panel and front
program, with one pointer and keyboard. The user's panel starts on their
Home folder, and Special ▸ Folders… adds, removes and reorders the user's
folder pages.

The GitHub release is another monolith built from this release's split
packages for computers whose updater is older than 1.18.30.
