ZacOS 9 1.18.20 brings Silver Coat Dark, a true dark mode for the desktop.

Appearance now offers Silver Coat Dark beside Silver Coat. It turns the
window chrome, menu bar, menus, panels, lists, text fields, buttons,
checkboxes, tabs, scroll bars and desktop dark, with white text that stays
readable on selections and coloured blocks. Icons, wallpapers and imported
theme artwork keep their own colours.

The Theme Editor's Dark Palette now makes a theme dark all the way through,
and Qt applications started while Silver Coat Dark is active follow it.
