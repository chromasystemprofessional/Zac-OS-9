Themes show Mac OS 9's standard icons again: the Apple menu icon, and
the standard folder, disk, Trash and document icons.

The System Folder copied from Old Dog's startup disk lost its resource
forks, because the kernel's HFS driver exposes only data forks. The copy
now reads the disk image directly and keeps every resource fork, and
older copies are repaired in place. Apple Platinum and the themes that
share its artwork no longer keep the theme file's document icon.

Themes already installed rebuild themselves the next time the
Appearance panel is opened.
