The Theme Installer now uses your Mac OS 9 System Folder, and can copy it
out of Old Dog for you.

Choose a Mac OS 9 theme file in the Theme Installer, or drop it onto the
System Folder. If ZacOS can't find your Mac OS 9 System Folder, it offers
to copy it from Old Dog's disk (quit Old Dog first). From it the theme
gets its Apple menu icon, its desktop picture, its alert sounds and its
fonts.

Themes installed before this release rebuild themselves the next time the
Appearance panel is opened: they gain the icons, pictures and sounds an
older ZacOS could not decode. Desktop pictures in any format work: a
PICT, JPEG, GIF, TIFF or BMP becomes a PNG for the desktop.

A disk's icon on the desktop now dims while its folder is open, as Mac OS 9
drew it.
