ZacOS 9 now states what it has always done: it does not track you.

A new Privacy page in ZacOS Help, and PRIVACY.md in the source, promise no
telemetry, analytics, online account, advertising ID, device fingerprint
or first-party server. They list every network connection ZacOS makes and
what it sends — Software Update, Software, Web search, local network
browsing and the services you choose.

A new test fails if a known tracking or analytics endpoint appears, or if
the update, Software or web-search code contacts an address the policy does
not list.
