The hard disk and Apple menu icons now come out right in Mac OS 9
themes.

A theme that does not supply its own hard disk art now keeps ZacOS's
grey disk, instead of showing an icon an older extraction left behind.
Rebuilding a theme also clears icons it no longer supplies.

The menu-bar Apple is drawn from the theme's fitted 20x15 icon when it
has one, so its bottom row is no longer clipped.
