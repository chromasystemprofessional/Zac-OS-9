After Dark screen savers can now be imported.

Open Control Panels ▸ Screen Saver, choose After Dark, and click Import…
Pick your own After Dark disk image, a folder of modules, or an archive of
them; ZacOS does not include the modules or fonts. The imported modules
appear in the list with a live preview. Not every module can run, the saver
is silent, and games show their attract screen. Each screen runs its own
copy, so a module uses one processor core per screen.

Hint Bubbles now explain Get Info's icon: ⌘C copies it, and ⌘V pastes a
picture from the Clipboard as the item's icon. New Tricks, Fetch Updates,
Bloodhound (Search), Old Dog, Screen Snapshot and the other newer menu
items have hints too.
