ZacOS 9 1.18.14 fixes sound sets.

DrawingBoard Sounds and other older sets now appear in Sound Sets
instead of being reported as an error. Their window, menu, checkbox,
Trash and drag sounds are imported. A screenshot or read-me beside the
sets is ignored rather than reported as a broken set.

The Appearance folder is now called Sound Sets, as on Mac OS 9; an
existing Sound Themes folder is renamed with its sets kept.

Dropping a sound set into Appearance > Sound Sets now keeps it there
instead of importing it as an appearance theme.
