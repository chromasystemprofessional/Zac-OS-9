ZacOS 9 1.18.10 makes Mac OS 9 themes render much closer to the
original, and keeps Old Dog running.

Appearance themes now use their own frame recipes. Push buttons take the
theme's frme bevel or its named pxm# 9-slice, title-bar controls draw at
their native size with the black control rails, and the document frame
rails and grow box come from the theme. Scrollbars use the theme's
enabled or disabled track, its single or lower-right arrows and its
normal or proportional thumb. Re-import a theme to pick up the new art.

Classic (Old Dog) no longer opens SheepShaver's audio, whose SDL callback
crashed here, and a normal launch leaves the Classic bridge dormant. The
emulator now stays up through startup instead of crashing after the
guest begins.
