ZacOS 9 1.18.18 keeps other programs' window sizes and loads ~/.Xresources.

LXTerminal, XTerm, galculator, Featherpad and other programs now open at
the size they ask for — their own default, their saved size or their
preferences — instead of being stretched to almost fill the screen. New
main windows still open inside the desktop strip, so the desktop and its
icons stay in view.

X11 programs also get the person's ~/.Xresources at start-up: XTerm's
colours and geometry now apply on its first launch, without running
xrdb -merge by hand.

Imported themes' frame rails and title-bar caps now line up with the
window edge, including themes already on disk; re-importing is not needed.
