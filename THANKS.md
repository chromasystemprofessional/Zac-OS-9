# Thanks

ZacOS 9 is a one-person project with a lot of help from other people. This page
is the record of that help. If your name is here and you would rather it were
not, open an issue and I will remove it.

## Brought ZacOS 9 to a wider audience

- **Felix Ding** ([@felixding](https://github.com/felixding)) posted ZacOS 9 to
  [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50189981) on 22 October
  2026. The post reached more than 50 points and 37 comments and brought the
  project its first large audience. Felix also filed five early issues
  ([#22](https://github.com/chromasystemprofessional/Zac-OS-9/issues/22),
  [#23](https://github.com/chromasystemprofessional/Zac-OS-9/issues/23),
  [#31](https://github.com/chromasystemprofessional/Zac-OS-9/issues/31),
  [#32](https://github.com/chromasystemprofessional/Zac-OS-9/issues/32) and
  [#34](https://github.com/chromasystemprofessional/Zac-OS-9/issues/34)),
  including fractional scaling, touch and the UTM install.
- **Liam Proven** ([@lproven](https://github.com/lproven)) wrote
  [“ZacOS 9 and Mavernix: Two classic Apple desktops find their Wayland to
  Linux”](https://www.theregister.com/os-platforms/2026/10/27/zacos-9-and-mavernix-two-classic-apple-desktops-find-their-wayland-to-linux/5305710)
  in The Register on 27 October 2026. He also starred the repository and has
  followed the project since.
- **The Hacker News community** — the people who tried it, asked hard
  questions and sent the project on to others.

## Built with

ZacOS 9 stands on Debian, Wayland and wlroots, Qt 6, Mesa, PipeWire, CUPS,
Samba and Netatalk, SheepShaver, and the many libraries and translators behind
them. The Mac OS 8 and 9 interface is the design language; no Apple code,
fonts, icons or sounds are included.
