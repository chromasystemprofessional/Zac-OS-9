Networks, file sharing and Bluetooth

Getting online

Open Control Panels ▸ TCP/IP. Choose Wi-Fi in "Connect via" and your network in the Network pop-up, or plug in a cable. The Network module in The Collar shows whether you're connected and turns Wi-Fi on or off.

Connecting to other computers

Zac menu ▸ Neighbourhood lists the computers near you that share files: Macs (AFP, including Mac OS 9's own File Sharing) and Windows or Linux computers (SMB). Double-click one, give your name and password if asked, and choose the volumes to connect to. They appear on the desktop like disks.

Servers you connected to are listed in the Zac menu's Recent Servers. To disconnect, select the volume and choose Special ▸ Eject, or drag it to the Trash.

Sharing your folders

Open Control Panels ▸ File Sharing and click Start. Other computers, old Macs included, can then connect to yours. Choose which folders to share, and who may use them, with File ▸ Get Info ▸ Sharing in the Sniffer. The File Sharing module in The Collar turns sharing on and off and shows who is connected.

Bluetooth

Open Control Panels ▸ Bluetooth, put your device in pairing mode, and choose it when it appears in the list. The Bluetooth module in The Collar turns Bluetooth on and off.
