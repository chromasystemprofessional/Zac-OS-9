Printing

Choosing your printer

Choose Zac menu ▸ Printer Picker. It lists the printers this computer knows, and those it has found on your network. Click one, and documents you print go to it. The Printer module in The Collar switches between printers too.

If your printer isn't listed, turn it on and connect it to this computer or your network. Settings… in Printer Picker adds a printer by hand.

Printing documents

  • In a program, choose File ▸ Print (⌘-P) and use its Print dialog.
  • In the Sniffer, select documents and choose File ▸ Print (⌘-P). PDFs, text files and pictures go straight to your printer. Other documents must be opened and printed from their own programs.

Desktop printers

Select a printer in Printer Picker and click Create Desktop Printer. Its icon appears on the desktop:

  • Drag documents onto it to print them on that printer.
  • Double-click it to open its print queue.

The print queue

A printer's queue window lists the documents waiting to print. Select one and click Hold to keep it from printing until you click Resume, or Remove to cancel it. Stop Printing pauses the whole printer, for example to change paper, and Start Printing sets it going again. You can also drop documents onto the window to print them.
