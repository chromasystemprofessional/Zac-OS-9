Working in the Sniffer

The Sniffer is the program that draws the desktop and the windows of your disks and folders. Its menus (File, Edit, View, Special and Help) are in the menu bar whenever you click the desktop or one of its windows.

Disks and folders

Zacintosh HD, at the top right of the desktop, holds Applications, the System Folder and your Home folder. Your home folder holds your own Documents, Downloads, Pictures and so on. Disks you plug in, and network volumes you connect to, appear on the desktop too.

Double-click a disk or folder to open its window. ⌘-↑ opens the folder that encloses the one in front; Option-⌘-↑ also closes the window you were in, and so does Option-double-clicking an icon. ⌘-click a window's title for a menu of the folders it's inside.

Views

Each window can show its contents as Icons, as Buttons (open with one click) or as List; choose from the View menu. Each window remembers its view.

  • In icon view, Clean Up lines icons up on the grid, and Arrange sorts them by name, date, size, kind or label.
  • In list view, click a column heading to sort by it, and click it again to reverse the order. Drag a heading to move the column, or the line between two to widen it. Click a folder's triangle to see what's in it without opening it.
  • View Options… sets icon size, grid snapping, Keep Arranged, and which columns a list shows. Edit ▸ Preferences sets the standard views and spring-loaded folders.
  • ⌘-drag inside a window to scroll it in any direction with the hand.

Pop-up windows

Choose View ▸ as Pop-up Window to keep a folder you use often at the bottom of the screen as a tab with its name. Click the tab to open the window above it; click the tab again, or anywhere else, to close it. Drag the tab along the bottom of the screen to move it. To drop something in, drag it onto the tab and hold it there: the window springs open. To make it an ordinary window again, choose View ▸ as Window, or drag its tab up off the bottom of the screen. Resize an open pop-up window by its top corners.

Icons

  • Click an icon to select it; Shift-click to add more, or drag a box around several. Type the first letters of a name to select it.
  • Click a selected icon's name to rename it.
  • File ▸ Get Info (⌘-I) shows an item's size, kind, where it is and its comments; Get Info ▸ Sharing shows who may read or change it.
  • File ▸ Label colours an item. Label names and colours can be changed in Edit ▸ Preferences.

The Trash

Drag items to the Trash, or press ⌘-Delete, to throw them away. They stay there until you choose Special ▸ Empty Trash…. To take something back out, select it in the Trash and choose File ▸ Put Away (⌘-Y). Dragging a network volume to the Trash disconnects it. Empty Trash asks first; to stop it asking, select the Trash, choose File ▸ Get Info and turn off Warn before emptying. It still asks before uninstalling programs.

Toy Box

The Toy Box (Zac menu ▸ Control Panels ▸ Toy Box) is a window of big buttons for the programs, documents and folders you use most. It shows the items in System Folder ▸ Toy Box Items; folders there whose names start with a bullet (•) become pages.
