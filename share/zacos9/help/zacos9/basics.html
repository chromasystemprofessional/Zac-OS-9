Desktop basics

The keys

ZacOS uses the Mac's names for the keys. ⌘ (Command) is the Command key on a Mac keyboard and the Windows key on a PC keyboard. Option is the Alt key. Shortcuts such as ⌘-C (Copy) and ⌘-W (Close) work in every ZacOS window, and in other programs too.

The menu bar

The menu bar along the top of the screen always shows the menus of the program in front. At its left is the Zac menu, which is always there. At its right are the clock, the keyboard menu (if you use more than one keyboard layout) and the Application menu, which names the program in front and lists the others.

Click a menu to open it, then click a command. You can also hold the mouse button down, drag to the command and let go.

Windows

  • Move a window by dragging its title bar. Hold ⌘ while dragging to move a window behind others without bringing it forward.
  • Close it with the close box at the left of the title bar, or ⌘-W. Option-click the close box to close all of that program's windows.
  • Collapse it into its title bar with the collapse box at the right (or double-click the title bar, if that's turned on in Appearance). Option-click collapses all of the program's windows.
  • Zoom it with the zoom box: a Sniffer window grows to fit its contents. Option-click the zoom box to fill the screen.
  • Resize it by dragging the resize box at the bottom right.

Contextual menus

Control-click, or right-click, an icon, a window's background or the desktop for a menu of the things you can do there. A document's menu includes Print, which prints it as File ▸ Print does.

Leaving

Choose Special ▸ Sleep, Restart or Shut Down when the Sniffer is in front. Special ▸ Log Out (⇧⌘-Q) asks your programs to quit, then returns to the login window.
