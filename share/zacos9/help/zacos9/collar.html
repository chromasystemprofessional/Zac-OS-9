The Collar

The Collar is the strip of small buttons at the bottom left of the screen. Each button, or module, opens a menu of quick settings.

  • Sound: the volume, mute, and which speakers or headphones to use.
  • Sound Set: the sounds ZacOS plays for windows and menus.
  • Network: whether you're connected, and Wi-Fi on or off.
  • Bluetooth: turn Bluetooth on or off.
  • Resolution and Brightness: one of each for every display.
  • Power: the battery's charge, and Sleep.
  • Media: what a music or video player is playing, with Play/Pause and Next and Previous Track. It appears while a player is open.
  • File Sharing: turn file sharing on or off, and see who is connected. It appears once File Sharing is set up.
  • Keychain: lock and unlock the keychains where programs keep passwords.
  • Phone: with KDE Connect, your phone's battery, sending files, finding it.

Using it

  • Click the close box at its left end to fold The Collar away to its tab; click the tab to bring it back.
  • Drag the tab at its right end to show more or fewer modules; the arrows scroll through the rest.
  • ⌘-drag anywhere on it to move it up or down the edge of the screen.
  • The Collar control panel shows or hides it and sets a key to do so.
