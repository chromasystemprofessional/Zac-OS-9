Thanks and Credits

ZacOS 9 is a one-person project with a lot of help from other people. Thank you to everyone below. If your name is here and you would rather it were not, open an issue and it will be removed.

Everyone else

Thank you to everyone who forked the repository, downloaded the ISO or the Debian package, installed ZacOS 9 and tried it on real hardware. Thank you, too, to the people who wrote kind words in the Hacker News thread and elsewhere; they were read.

Built with

ZacOS 9 stands on Debian, Wayland and wlroots, Qt 6, Mesa, PipeWire, CUPS, Samba and Netatalk, SheepShaver, and the many libraries and translators behind them. The Mac OS 8 and 9 interface is the design language; no Apple code, fonts, icons or sounds are included.
