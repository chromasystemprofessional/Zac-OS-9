Learn ZacOS

Welcome to ZacOS 9. These lessons show you around, a few minutes at a time. Choose where you'd like to start.

I used a Mac before

A five-minute tour for anyone who knew Mac OS 7, 8 or 9: what works the way you remember, what has a new name, what's new, and how to bring your old Mac along.

New to computers?

Start with Help ▸ Mouse Practice: a short game that teaches pointing, clicking, double-clicking and dragging. Then read Desktop basics in ZacOS Help: windows, menus and the keys.

For children

Mouse Practice has a Puppy School version, with simpler words and bigger targets: choose Practice ▸ Puppy School. It's on to begin with for children whose accounts use Puppy Mode.
