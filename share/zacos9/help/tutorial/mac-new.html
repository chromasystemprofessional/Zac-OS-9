What's new since Mac OS 9

ZacOS looks like the classic Mac, but underneath it's a modern system. Here's what it adds.

Today's programs

  • Zac menu ▸ New Tricks is a catalog of programs to install: web browsers, office suites, photo editors, games. Choose one and click Install.
  • Many programs from elsewhere are drawn in the Platinum look too.
  • Zac menu ▸ Windows Installer installs a Windows program from its installer (.exe).
  • To remove a program, drag it from Applications to the Trash and empty it.

Staying up to date

Zac menu ▸ Fetch Updates checks for new versions of ZacOS and for system updates, security fixes among them, and installs them.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

  • Choose a Wi-Fi network in Control Panels ▸ TCP/IP, or turn Wi-Fi on and off from the Network module in The Collar.
  • Pair keyboards, mice and headphones in Control Panels ▸ Bluetooth.
  • With KDE Connect on your phone, The Collar's Phone module shows its battery and sends it files.

People and children

  • Control Panels ▸ Users gives everyone their own account and password, and can let guests log in.
  • Puppy Mode is the simple desktop for children, like the Simple Finder: the Sniffer's menus keep only the essential commands. Turn it on for someone in Users ▸ Open….

Other new things

  • Screen Sharing (Control Panels) lets you see and use another ZacOS computer's screen, with its owner's permission.
  • Nap Time can start up and shut down the computer at set times, and Screen Saver can ask for your password when you come back.
  • Zac menu ▸ Screen Snapshot: drag across part of the screen to save a picture of it.
  • File ▸ Search Internet in the Sniffer searches the web.
