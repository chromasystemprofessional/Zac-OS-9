Bring your old Mac along

Your old disks, programs and Macs all still have a place here.

Your old programs: Old Dog

Old Dog runs Mac OS 7.5 to 9 in a window, so your old programs and games keep working. You supply a Macintosh ROM file and a system disk from a Mac you own, in a folder named Classic in your home folder, then choose Zac menu ▸ Old Dog. Your home folder appears on the old Mac's desktop as a disk named Unix. How to set up Old Dog

Your old disks

Plug in a Mac disk, or double-click a Mac disk image (.dsk, .img, .toast or .cdr), and it appears on the desktop like any other disk. Mac disks open read-only, so nothing on them can be changed by accident: drag what you need onto Zacintosh HD.

Your old Mac

  • To get files from a Mac on your network, turn on its File Sharing, then choose Zac menu ▸ Neighbourhood here. Macs sharing over TCP/IP are listed, and so are older Macs that share only over AppleTalk.
  • To let a Mac get files from here, open Control Panels ▸ File Sharing and click Start. The Mac connects to it like any other file server, from its Chooser or Network Browser.
